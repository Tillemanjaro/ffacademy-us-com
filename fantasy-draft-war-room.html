Draft Day War Room

You're on the clock in a live 10-team draft. Nine AI general managers are drafting around you — and they will punish sloppy picks. Can you draft like a pro?

Your Mission

Make 6 picks from the 5-spot in a snake draft. After every pick, your draft coach grades the reasoning behind it — not just the name. To earn an A, you'll need to apply the four habits of value-based drafting:

📊 Draft tiers, not rankingsPlayers inside a tier are interchangeable. The costly mistakes happen at tier breaks.
📉 Scarcity drives valueTake the position that falls off a cliff first. The Drop-off Radar shows you where.
🔥 Don't chase runsWhen a position run starts, panic is contagious. Check the math before you follow the herd.
💰 Respect the marketADP is the market price. Reaching 10+ picks early burns value you can never get back.
