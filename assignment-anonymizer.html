🎓 Assignment Anonymizer

100% private — runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, sent, or stored anywhere. Paste or drop student work, list the names to remove, and get a clean copy you can safely paste into any AI for grading. A private key file lets you re-attach grades to the right student afterward.

1 · Who to anonymize

Paste your class roster — one student per line. Accepts “First Last”, “Last, First”, or full names. This is the most reliable way to catch every name.

2 · Also auto-scrub

How it decides what’s a name
  • Every roster name is matched in full, and as first-only / last-only, case-insensitively.
  • “Last, First” entries are also matched as “First Last”.
  • Possessives are handled — Jordan’s essay → Student A’s essay.
  • Auto-scrub patterns run after roster names, so headers and emails are caught even for unlisted students.
  • Nothing is ever sent off your device — view source and confirm.
