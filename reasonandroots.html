The Question

The human ache for meaning is the door.

Before doctrine, there is longing: What is true? Why am I here? Does God come close?

The center is not an idea. It is a person.

03

Cross

Hard questions are carried into Christ's wounds.

Every honest doubt, every unanswered cry—Christ did not bypass them. He entered them. The cross is not the silencing of the question; it is God absorbing it.

The wound is real. So is the one who bears it.

04

Resurrection

Death is not the final word.

The tomb was empty—not as metaphor, but as fact. History records a movement that would not stop even when its founder was executed. Something changed them.

Reason follows the evidence. The roots go deeper than doubt.
